WHAT IS TRACEBACK?

TraceBack introduces a digital Lost & Found System to ease the process. It allows users to search for lost items and administrators to store and manage item details like date found, location, and characteristics. By automating the process, the system improves efficiency, increases recovery rates, and enhances user satisfaction, making it a practical solution for organizations and public spaces.
